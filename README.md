# Medical-Care-Analytics-Dashboard-Power-BI

An interactive **Medical Care Analytics Dashboard** built with Microsoft Power BI to analyze patients, hospitals, doctors, medical conditions, procedures, medications, billing, and insurance coverage.

The project transforms raw healthcare data into meaningful **KPIs, trends, and actionable insights** through an interactive multi-page dashboard.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to provide a comprehensive view of healthcare operations and patient-related data through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard helps explore:

- Patient demographics and characteristics
- Medical conditions and clinical patterns
- Hospital and doctor performance
- Medical procedures and medications
- Billing and insurance coverage
- Healthcare costs and payment trends
- Operational and clinical KPIs

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This dashboard was designed to help healthcare stakeholders:

- Monitor overall healthcare performance
- Understand patient demographics and medical conditions
- Analyze hospital and doctor activity
- Track billing and insurance coverage
- Identify major medical conditions and procedures
- Analyze healthcare costs and payment behavior
- Discover trends that can support data-driven decisions

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of the healthcare dataset.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Patients
- Total Billing
- Total Hospitals
- Average Satisfaction Rating

**Main analysis:**
- Billing by Department
- Patients by City
- Billing by Gender
- Insurance Distribution
- Encounters by Month

---

### 2. Patients

Focuses on patient demographics and medical characteristics.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Patients
- Average Age
- Male Patients
- Female Patients

**Main analysis:**
- Patients by Age Group
- Patients by Gender
- Patients by Medical Condition
- Patient Distribution by City
- Patient Demographics

---

### 3. Hospital & Doctors

Analyzes healthcare facilities, doctors, and patient encounters.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Hospitals
- Total Doctors
- Average Encounters per Hospital

**Main analysis:**
- Top Doctors by Encounters
- Encounters by Hospital
- Patients by Department
- Hospital Rating Distribution
- Hospital Performance

### 4. Financial & Billing

Provides an overview of healthcare financial performance.

**Key KPIs:**

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard enables users to identify patterns such as:

- Which medical conditions are most common
- Which departments handle the highest number of procedures
- Which hospitals and doctors have the highest encounter volumes
- How healthcare billing changes over time
- The relationship between patient payments and insurance coverage
- Patient distribution across different age groups and genders
- Which medications and procedures are most frequently used
- Areas with higher rates of abnormal laboratory results
